MINECRAFT: STORY MODE

GENRE: episodic, point-and-click
SCORE: 10/10

MINECRAFT: STORY MODE is a game using the MINECRAFT universe for an eposodic point-and-click game. It focuses on the protagonist JESSE and his/her friends (OLVIA, PETRA, AXEL, and LUKAS) going through various adventures in their world and EVENTUALLY beyond. It utilizes the MINECRAFT universe in a unique way, keeping FEATURES and CONCEPTS from the base game INTACT while putting a FUN twist on new items or places. JESSE's group has a fun DYNAMIC, allowing for fresh character development between THEM and many ANTAGONISTS. It is also VERY FUNNY in general.

DEADLOCK

GENRE: third-person shooter, multiplayer online battle arena
SCORE: 100/10

It's STILL in closed testing beta as of writing, but I have been given an invite a while back and oh my god if Lobotomy Corporation was like crack then this game is heroin. The worldbuilding and character designs are so good from the bits and pieces Valve has given us already, and the gameplay is still super fun despite it being my first ever MOBA. They're receptive to feedback and complaints, plus the community is extremely funny and entertaining. I cannot wait for more updates along the line, and for this game to be fully polished.
