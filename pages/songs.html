THE HANDS THAT THIEVE

GENRE: ska punk, punk rock
SCORE: 10/10

STREETLIGHT MANIFESTO IS FROM NEW JERSEY!!!!!!!!! The Hands That Thieve is an album released in April 2013. I enjoy the long intros and various horn instruments (I presume they are?) from the band, which is of course prevalent in this album. My personal favorite track from The Hands That Thieve is "Your Day Will Come" thanks to the almost upbeat tone, compared to the lyrics in some sections. I recommend this band and album for a fun listening experience. and that they're from new jersey this is so amazing to me dude.

EAST SIDE MILITIA

GENRE: electronica, industrial rock
SCORE: 100/10

I listened to this album for the first time during sophmore year throughout two school days. I have been invested in Chemlab ever since. The music is extremely loud, yes, but there's a certain charm to the mashups and snippets taken from other places. My personal favorite track from this album is "Exile on Mainline" thanks to the intro, a snippet from a source I don't know yet. I heavily recommend Chemlab
